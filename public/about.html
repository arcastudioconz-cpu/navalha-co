The Barber

Meet Eduardo.

One barber. One chair. A studio built so the person in the seat has all of the attention, all of the time.

Eduardo started Navalha & Co with a simple frustration: great haircuts shouldn't feel rushed. So he built the opposite of a busy shop — a private, appointment-only room where there's no next customer waiting, no clippers buzzing in the background, and no compromise on the finish.

Every appointment begins with a real conversation about what you want and what actually suits you. The result is a cut that's deliberate, tailored and repeatable — not a guess.
