The Barber

Meet Eduardo.

One barber. One chair. A private studio built behind home. A dream of creating a premium barbering experience.

Navalha & Co was born from a dream. From a young age, Eduardo knew he wanted to become a barber. With dedication and passion, he turned that dream into reality by building his own private studio behind his home—a simple, organised space filled with good energy and genuine hospitality.

Unlike busy barbershops, where long queues and rushed appointments are the norm, Navalha & Co was created to offer a more relaxed and personal experience. Here, there’s no pressure to finish quickly and no next client waiting at the door.

Soft music plays in the background, the atmosphere is calm and welcoming, and every client receives the time and attention they deserve.

Every appointment begins with a genuine conversation about what you’re looking for and what truly suits you. More than just a haircut, the goal is to create a style that complements your appearance, reflects your personality, and leaves you feeling confident.

At Navalha & Co, every haircut is delivered with care, attention to detail, and pride in the craft.
